Understanding Pre-Mover Factors

📊 Accumulation

Net BUY value from bulk deals over 5 trading days. Large accumulation signals insider/institutional buying.

🔑 Insider Activity

Promoter SAST/PIT transactions and pledge changes. Buying accumulation or release signals are bullish.

📈 F&O Buildup

OTM-call OI increase in options. Signals bullish positioning before expected move.

🎯 Catalyst

Tier-1 policy events or earnings announcements matching the ticker's sector.

🔊 Volume Anomaly

Unusual volume spikes (z≥2.0) without a matching high-α signal — hidden accumulation.

💡 News Catalyst

Multiple moderate-to-high alpha signals in 7 days. Density + quality = momentum building.
