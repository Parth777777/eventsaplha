How this score is built
factor stack · honest disclosure of inputs & gaps

PreMoverScore is a weighted blend of eight leading-indicator factors. Per horizon, the same factor stack is reweighted — 1D leans on intraday volume + OI build-up, 20D leans on valuation + policy catalyst.

  • accumulation — net BUY value across bulk & block deals over the last 5 trading days, log-scaled.
  • insider — directional sum of promoter SAST/PIT acquire-vs-dispose events and pledge changes (last 30d).
  • oi_buildup — F&O unusual-OI score on OTM calls minus puts, last 24h.
  • vol_divergence — volume z-score > 2 in last 3d without a high-α news signal in 48h.
  • catalyst — Tier-1 RBI/SEBI/MoF/PIB/CBDT policy event mapped to the ticker (direct) or its sector (fan-out).
  • valuation — PE / PB discount vs. sector median (only computed for surviving candidates to keep the call cheap).
  • low_attention — signal count over 30d below sector p25.
  • sector_tailwind — sector average alpha in top quartile, while the ticker's own alpha lags the sector mean.

Hard filters before scoring: excluded if forensic band is suspicious / likely_manipulated, if the entry-price has moved >5% in 5d (already surged), or if no qualifying source signal exists.

Honest gaps. The repo doesn't carry an intraday OHLCV table, so the volume divergence factor approximates with the volume_anomalies z-score. Backtested hit-rate for PreMoverScore itself isn't published yet — predictions emitted by the existing tracker will start scoring this surface forward from launch.
