Our edge — the honest version

5 things we do that retail tools don't
1. Subject-verified events. Nuvama doesn't get alpha for rating Tata Steel. TCS doesn't get credit for Tata Consumer news. We require the ticker (or its short-name) to appear in the article body — eliminates the #1 false-signal source.
2. Volume confirmation gate. OBV divergence detected → 0.75× penalty even on great news. Real accumulation + volume surge → 1.35× boost. We trust the tape over the tweet.
3. Forensic skepticism. Coordinated article campaigns, insider sell streaks, promoter pledge jumps, unexplained pre-news volume — each one down-weights the signal. We try hard NOT to recommend a pump.
4. Fundamental gating. A high-alpha event on a stock with negative cashflow + 30% promoter pledge + declining revenue gets dropped, not promoted. Look for the F-score chip on each card.
5. Curation discipline. Sector cap (max 2/sector), mega-cap cap (max 2 in any slate). No "5 IT stocks today" slop.
What we're NOT. We don't have Bloomberg-grade data, tick-level feeds, or proprietary models. We win against picking off Twitter/CNBC, not against a quant fund. Position size accordingly.
