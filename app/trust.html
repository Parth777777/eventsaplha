How we compute trust

  1. Source tiering. BSE/NSE/SEBI filings → 1.0 weight. ET/Mint/Reuters → 0.85. Aggregators → 0.6. Unknown blogs → 0.3.
  2. Cross-source confirmation. A signal can't fire above α=65 unless ≥2 independent Tier-1/2 sources confirm within 6 hours, or it's a direct exchange filing.
  3. Forensic gate. Every event is scored across 7 fingerprints (anonymous sourcing, contradictions, pressure language, recycled phrasing, etc.). Bands: clean / unverified / suspicious / likely_manipulated.
  4. Prediction tracker. Every signal logs a forecast for 1D/3D/20D. After the horizon passes, the actual return is fetched from yfinance and the prediction is marked hit or miss — immutably.
  5. No retroactive editing. Predictions are append-only. We can't go back and change what we said.

Disclaimers

Not investment advice. Tickwave publishes statistical signals derived from public news + market data. It is research tooling, not registered advisory. Do your own diligence and consult a SEBI-registered investment advisor before trading.

Data sources. NSE/BSE corporate filings (public), SEBI disclosures (public), RBI press releases (public), 20+ Indian financial RSS feeds, yfinance market data. Every event card links back to its primary source — click through to verify.

Manipulation reporting. If a signal here looks fake or coordinated, click the forensics badge on its card and submit feedback. False positives + false negatives are reviewed weekly.
