Privacy Policy

Last updated · 2026-04-27

This policy explains what Tickwave collects, why, how long we keep it, and your rights over it. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023.

What we collect

  • Account data: email, hashed password, display name, account tier.
  • Product data: watchlists, alerts, paper-trade entries, screener queries you save.
  • Telemetry: request paths, response codes, latency, browser type, IP address (truncated for analytics).
  • Notification config: Telegram chat id, Discord webhook URL, push tokens — only if you opt in.

We do not collect government IDs, bank details, or biometrics. Payment is handled by a third-party processor; Tickwave never sees raw card numbers.

Why we collect it

  • To provide the service you signed up for (watchlists, alerts, screeners, portfolio tracking).
  • To enforce tier limits and bill subscriptions.
  • To debug errors, monitor abuse, and prioritise improvements.
  • To send transactional notifications you have explicitly enabled.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is active, plus 90 days after closure for billing reconciliation.
  • Product data (watchlists, alerts): deleted on account closure.
  • Telemetry: retained 30 days, then aggregated.
  • Backups: rolling 14-day window.

Who we share it with

We do not sell user data. We share narrow slices only with:

  • The hosting provider that runs the database and application servers.
  • The payment processor, if you subscribe.
  • Telegram / Discord / push services, only when you have explicitly configured those channels.
  • Law-enforcement agencies, only in response to a valid legal order.

Your rights under DPDP

  • Access: request a copy of your stored data.
  • Correction: ask us to fix inaccurate fields.
  • Erasure: close your account and have product data removed.
  • Withdrawal of consent: revoke specific channels (push, Telegram, Discord) without losing your account.
  • Grievance: contact our data protection officer below.

Cookies & local storage

We use one session cookie to keep you logged in and local storage for theme preference. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Data sources we ingest

Headlines and news content displayed on Tickwave are aggregated from public RSS feeds and news APIs. We store article URL, title, and a short summary; we do not republish full bodies. Stock fundamentals are fetched from third-party providers and public exchange filings.

Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Connections are served over HTTPS. Internal services authenticate to one another with rotating tokens. No system is unbreakable; if we discover a breach affecting your data, we will notify you within 72 hours and disclose the scope.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be highlighted in the dashboard and require re-acknowledgement at next login. Minor wording fixes will simply update the date stamp.
