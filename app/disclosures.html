Disclosures & SEBI Compliance
Version 1.0 · Effective 17 May 2026

1. Who we are

AlphaEvent (also referred to as Tickwave) is a quantitative research and analytics platform for the Indian equity market. We aggregate, classify, and surface public information from NSE and BSE corporate filings, SEBI disclosures, RBI press releases, exchange announcements, and financial news in real time.

Important: AlphaEvent / Tickwave is NOT a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA). We are research tooling, not a personalised advisory service.

2. What we publish

  • Event signals derived from corporate filings, earnings, M&A, policy, and insider activity.
  • Statistical scores (alpha score, hit rate, conviction tier) computed from historical event-outcome data.
  • Aggregated public information: shareholding patterns, bulk & block deals, SEBI insider disclosures, credit-rating actions.
  • AI-generated factual summaries of long-form filings (concall transcripts, investor presentations) for navigation purposes.

Methodology: see our public /methodology page for live hit-rate breakdowns by source, conviction tier, and time horizon.

3. What this is NOT

  • Not investment advice. Nothing on AlphaEvent constitutes a recommendation, solicitation, or offer to buy or sell any security.
  • Not personalised. We do not assess your financial situation, risk tolerance, investment objectives, or tax position.
  • Not a guarantee. Past signal performance does not guarantee future results. Hit rates are historical statistics, not predictions.
  • Not a substitute for due diligence. "Reference entry" and "indicative level" values are statistical artefacts, not analyst price targets.
  • Quant Playground tools are educational. Black-Scholes, Greeks, Monte Carlo, Kelly, payoff diagrams, and backtests compute theoretical values using standard models. Real markets diverge from model assumptions (fat tails, jumps, vol regime shifts). Outputs are for understanding the math — not orders, not signals, not advice.

4. Risk factors

Indian equities carry substantial risk. Specifically:

  • Volatility. Small- and mid-cap stocks can move 10–20% in a single session on news flow.
  • Liquidity. Many BSE-only and SME scrips have limited daily volume; executing at reference levels is not guaranteed.
  • Event signals can fail. Order wins, earnings beats, and rating upgrades do not always translate into price appreciation.
  • Data lag & error. Source feeds can be delayed or contain errors. We make best effort to flag discrepancies but cannot guarantee accuracy.
  • Regulatory risk. SEBI / RBI policy changes can materially affect the value of holdings without notice.

5. Consult a registered professional

Before acting on any information presented on AlphaEvent, consult a SEBI-registered Research Analyst or Investment Adviser, and consider your own risk tolerance, time horizon, and tax situation. The official SEBI registry of intermediaries is available at sebi.gov.in/intermediaries.

6. Complaints & escalation

If you have a grievance about content or conduct on AlphaEvent, contact us first at aditya.j.thakker@gmail.com. If unresolved, you may escalate via SEBI's grievance redressal portal:

  • SEBI SCORES (scores.sebi.gov.in) — the official investor grievance platform.
  • SMART-ODR (smartodr.in) — Online Dispute Resolution for capital markets.

7. Data sources & attribution

We pull from: BSE Corporate Announcements API, NSE Corporate Announcements API, SEBI insider trading & SAST disclosures, RBI press releases, published financial news (RSS), yfinance for OHLCV, CRISIL / ICRA / CARE rating press releases, and company Investor Relations pages. We do not proxy, mirror, or republish content from paywalled or subscription services such as Bloomberg, Refinitiv, or research broker reports.
