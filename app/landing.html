Questions

The honest FAQ.

Is Tickwave a SEBI-registered investment advisor?

No. Tickwave publishes statistical research signals from public Indian market data (NSE/BSE filings, SEBI disclosures, RBI press, financial press). It is research tooling, not advice. Past performance ≠ future results. Always do your own due diligence.

Where does the data come from?

NSE/BSE corporate filings, SEBI/RBI press releases, 30+ financial news feeds (ET, Mint, MoneyControl, Bloomberg, Reuters, LiveMint), and curated F&O / volume data via official APIs.

How fresh are the signals?

Under 3 minutes from source publication to your screen on average. Policy events have a separate 2-hour cooldown so RBI/SEBI multi-circulars don't drown the feed.

What's the alpha score actually measuring?

An 11-factor regime-aware score from 0 to 100. It combines magnitude, confidence, event-type-by-regime weighting, sentiment, time decay, market-cap quality, sector momentum, relative strength, divergence bonus, narrative density, surprise, and flow.

Will my data be sold?

Never. Email is used for product comms only. No third-party data brokers. No ad networks.

When does this launch?

The core engine is already live and scoring signals 24×7. The product wrapper for retail is in private beta — first 500 waitlist members get keys the day the doors open.
