What you should know before you subscribe
disclosures · data sourcing · refunds

Not investment advice. Tickwave is a market intelligence and analytics tool. Outputs (alpha scores, predictions, signals, ratings) are model-generated views derived from public news and market data. They are not investment recommendations, research-analyst opinions, or solicitations to trade any security. Decide based on your own analysis or licensed adviser.

Data sourcing. Fundamentals and quotes shown on this site are aggregated from third-party providers (e.g. Yahoo Finance) and public exchange disclosures (NSE / BSE). Prices may be delayed; corporate filings may lag the original release. We do not redistribute raw exchange feeds.

Cancellation & refunds. You can cancel any time from your account; access continues until the end of the current billing cycle. We do not offer pro-rata refunds for partial months.

Privacy. Watchlists, alerts, and portfolio entries are tied to your user id and stored on Tickwave servers. We do not sell user data to third parties. See privacy policy and terms of use.
