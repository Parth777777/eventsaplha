FAQ

How do payments work?

Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets) — same gateway your broker uses. Annual plans are billed once; monthly auto-renew via Razorpay subscriptions. Cancel anytime from Settings; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. Upgrade takes effect immediately and is pro-rated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep paid access until then.

Why two paid tiers?

Most retail traders need the full signal feed + a daily briefing — that's the Starter pitch. Active intraday/F&O traders also want pre-market positioning, instant alerts when their watchlist moves, and earnings-call summaries delivered within 30 min — that's Pro.

What do you mean by "AI research"?

An 8-section equity report composed from our scraped tables (signals, events, forensic flags, peers, fundamentals) with AI-written narrative for the investment thesis and risk factors. Every claim cites the source. Cached server-side for 24h so re-opening a report is instant.

Is AlphaEvent a broker or advisor?

Neither. We're a research and intelligence tool. No order execution; no portfolio management; not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. Use AlphaEvent to find ideas — execute and consult through your broker and a SEBI-RIA.

Can I get a refund?

Monthly plans are non-refundable but cancellable anytime. Annual plans — full refund within 7 days of purchase; pro-rated thereafter for the first 30 days. Email support@alphaevent.in.
