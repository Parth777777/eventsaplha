Inflation formula

Future cost = Today × (1 + inflation)years

Future buying power of today's amount = Today / (1 + inflation)years. This is the reason a ₹50,000/month lifestyle today will need ~₹1.6 lakh/month in 20 years just to maintain the same standard.

India inflation context

India's CPI inflation has averaged 5-6% over the last decade, with food inflation often higher (7-8%) and education/healthcare even higher (8-10%). Use 7% for retirement / healthcare planning, 9% for education.

FAQ

What inflation rate should I use?

General CPI 6-7%. Education 8-10%. Healthcare 8-9%. Real estate 5-6% in cities. Use the highest relevant rate for goal planning.

How do I beat inflation?

Invest in assets that historically beat inflation: equity (10-12% long-run), real estate (8% + rental), gold (8%). Avoid keeping more than 6 months of expenses in cash/FD.

Why does this matter for goal planning?

A ₹1 cr corpus today is worth ₹31 lakh in 20 years. Always inflate your goal before computing the required SIP — use the Goal SIP Calculator with the inflated target.
