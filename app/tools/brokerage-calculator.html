FAQ

Why so many separate charges?

Brokerage goes to broker; STT and stamp duty to govt; exchange & SEBI charges to exchange/regulator; GST is tax on services; DP charges to depository. All real, none avoidable.

Are full-service broker rates similar?

No — they typically charge 0.3-0.5% per side on delivery, which can be 10-20× the discount-broker total. Discount brokers won the retail mass market.

Does this work for F&O?

This calculator is for cash equity only. F&O has different rates — STT 0.0125% on sell-side options, exchange charges ~0.053% for futures, etc.
