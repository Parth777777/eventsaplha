PPF rules quick reference

  • Tenure: 15 years lock-in. Extendable in 5-year blocks indefinitely.
  • Deposit limit: Min ₹500/year, max ₹1.5 lakh/year (counts toward 80C).
  • Compounding: Annual. Interest credited 31 March every year on minimum balance between 5th and end of each month.
  • Tax: EEE — contribution 80C deductible, interest tax-free, maturity tax-free.
  • Partial withdrawal: From 7th year. Loan from 3rd year.

FAQ

PPF or ELSS — which is better for 80C?

Risk-averse: PPF (guaranteed 7.1% tax-free). Risk-tolerant + 15-yr horizon: ELSS (12% long-term but volatile, only 3-yr lock). Most balanced portfolios have both.

Can NRIs open PPF?

No new accounts. Existing accounts can continue until original 15-yr maturity, no extensions.

What if I deposit late in the financial year?

Interest is credited on the 5th-end-of-month minimum balance. Deposit before the 5th to maximize that month's interest.
