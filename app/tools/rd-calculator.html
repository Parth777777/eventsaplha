How RD compounding works

Each monthly deposit earns interest from the date it's deposited until maturity. Banks compound quarterly. Computation: simulate month-by-month — each deposit gets compounded for its remaining months until tenure ends.

FAQ

RD vs SIP — which is better?

RD gives guaranteed returns at lower rates (6-7%). SIP in equity is volatile but ~12% long-term. For long horizons (5+ yrs), SIP wins. For short conservative savings, RD.

Is RD interest taxable?

Yes — fully taxable at slab. Banks deduct 10% TDS if interest exceeds ₹40k/yr.

What if I miss an RD instalment?

Most banks charge a small penalty (~₹1-2 per ₹100 missed) and may close the account after 4-6 consecutive misses.
