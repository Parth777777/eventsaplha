How retirement math works

  1. Inflate today's monthly expense to retirement-time expense at your inflation rate.
  2. Compute corpus required to fund N years of inflated expenses, with the corpus itself growing at a safer post-retirement rate.
  3. Reverse-engineer the monthly SIP needed in your accumulation years at pre-retirement equity returns.
Future monthly expense = current × (1 + inflation)yrs to retire
Corpus ≈ FME × 12 × [1 − (1 + real_r)−N] / real_r   (annuity present value)

FAQ

Should I include EPF / NPS / PPF in the corpus?

Yes. Compute their expected maturity value at retirement and subtract from the required corpus before computing the gap-funding SIP.

Is 25× rule reliable?

25× annual expense is the "Trinity study" American thumb-rule. For India with higher inflation, 30-33× is safer.

What return should I assume post-retirement?

6-8% — corpus shifts to a debt-heavy mix (60% debt / 40% equity glide path). Don't assume 12% throughout retirement.
