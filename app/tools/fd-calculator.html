FD interest taxation

FD interest is fully taxable at your slab rate. Banks deduct 10% TDS if interest in a year exceeds ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for seniors). You still owe the full slab rate at filing — TDS is just upfront withholding.

For tax-efficient debt allocation, compare FD with debt mutual funds — both are taxed at slab post-April-2023, but MFs let you control realisation timing.

FAQ

Quarterly vs monthly compounding — does it matter?

Marginal. At 7.5% over 5 years on ₹1 lakh: quarterly ₹1.45 L, monthly ₹1.45 L (difference ~₹400). Pick whichever your bank offers.

What's the TDS impact?

10% TDS deducted by bank if interest > ₹40k/yr (₹50k senior). You owe the full slab on filing; TDS is upfront credit.

Are tax-saving FDs (5-year ELSS) worth it?

Section 80C deduction (₹1.5L) only available in old tax regime, AND interest is taxable. Compare with ELSS mutual fund — generally better post-tax for 5+ year horizon.
