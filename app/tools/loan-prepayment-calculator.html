Prepay vs invest — the decision rule

Prepay if your loan rate (after tax benefit) is higher than your expected after-tax investment return. For most retail borrowers, a 8.5% home loan with tax benefit ≈ 6-7% effective cost, vs equity MF returning 11-12% before tax / 10-11% after LTCG.

Verdict logic: "Invest" when alt-return > loan rate. "Prepay" otherwise. "Mixed" when both are close (within 1.5%) — split the prepayment.

FAQ

Should I prepay or invest in equity?

Mathematically, equity beats home loan ~70% of the time over 15 years. But prepaying gives certain savings; equity returns are risky. A balanced split (50/50) is reasonable for most.

Is there a prepayment penalty?

Floating-rate retail home loans: zero penalty (RBI rule). Fixed-rate: 2-4% penalty possible. Check sanction letter.

EMI reduction or tenure reduction after prepayment?

Tenure reduction saves more interest. Bankers default to EMI reduction; you have to explicitly ask for tenure reduction.
