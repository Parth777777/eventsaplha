How step-up SIP works

Each year, your monthly SIP increases by the step-up percentage. If you start at ₹10,000/month with a 10% step-up, year 2 is ₹11,000, year 3 is ₹12,100, and so on. The earlier years' contributions still compound the longest; the later years' larger contributions compound less but on bigger principal — both forces add up.

FV = Σ over each year y: SIPy × [((1+r)12(n-y+1) − 1) / r] × (1+r)

How much does a step-up actually add?

At 10%/year step-up vs flat, starting at ₹10k for 20 years at 12%: flat SIP gives ₹99 lakh; step-up gives ₹1.83 cr — roughly 85% more. Of course you also invested more (₹68 lakh vs ₹24 lakh), but the proportional gain is real.

FAQ

Do all mutual funds support step-up SIP?

Most AMCs allow registering an annual top-up at SIP creation. Some also let you increment via the mobile app. If yours doesn't, manually increase the SIP amount each year.

What step-up % is realistic?

Match your expected salary growth — typically 8-10% for India. Aggressive savers push to 15%.

Should step-up % equal inflation?

At minimum, yes — otherwise your real SIP shrinks. Ideally 2-4% above inflation so your real investment grows.
