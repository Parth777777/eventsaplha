HRA exemption formula (Section 10(13A))

The exempt HRA is the minimum of:

A. Actual HRA received from employer
B. Rent paid − 10% of basic salary
C. 50% of basic (metro) or 40% (non-metro)

Metros for HRA purposes: Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai. Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, etc. are non-metro under this section.

Documents you need

  • Rent receipts every month (or quarterly)
  • Landlord PAN if annual rent > ₹1,00,000
  • Rent agreement (recommended)
  • Bank statement showing rent paid (recommended for clean trail)

FAQ

Can I claim HRA while owning a house?

Yes, if you actually pay rent (e.g. living in a rented place in your work city while your owned house is elsewhere). The house and rented residence must be in different cities, or there must be a genuine reason.

Is HRA available in new tax regime?

No. HRA exemption is old regime only.

What if I pay rent to my parents?

Allowed, but parent must declare it as rental income on their return. Bank transfer (not cash) recommended. Watch for clubbing-of-income rules if spouse.
