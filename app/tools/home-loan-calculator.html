Tax benefits on home loan

  • Section 80C: Principal repayment up to ₹1.5 lakh / year (old regime only)
  • Section 24(b): Interest paid up to ₹2 lakh / year on self-occupied property (old regime only)
  • Section 80EEA: Additional ₹1.5 lakh interest deduction for affordable housing (eligibility conditions apply)

The new tax regime (default from FY 2024-25) does not allow home loan interest or principal deductions for self-occupied. Compare both regimes with the Income Tax Calculator before deciding.

Why interest dominates early payments

In the first year of a 20-year loan, only ~16% of each EMI goes to principal — the rest is interest on the still-large outstanding balance. By year 15, more than 60% of each EMI is principal. Prepaying in the first 5-7 years has the biggest impact — see Loan Prepayment Calculator.

FAQ

Floating or fixed rate?

Floating tracks the repo rate — falls when RBI cuts, rises when it hikes. Fixed locks the rate for a period (usually 2-5 yrs, then converts to floating). Floating is the default 95% of the time.

How much loan can I qualify for?

Banks typically allow EMI ≤ 50% of monthly take-home, and loan-to-value ≤ 80%. Use the Salary Calculator to confirm take-home.

Should I take a longer or shorter tenure?

Longer = lower EMI but higher total interest. Shorter = higher EMI but huge interest saving. A balanced choice: 20 years with intent to prepay aggressively in years 5-10.
