Gratuity formula

Gratuity = (Basic + DA) × 15 / 26 × Years of service

26 represents the number of working days in a month (excluding weekly off). 15 represents half-a-month of salary per completed year of service. Partial years above 6 months count as full year.

Eligibility & tax

  • Minimum tenure: 5 continuous years with the same employer (waived if employee dies / becomes disabled)
  • Tax-free cap: ₹20 lakh lifetime (across all employers) for private-sector. Govt employees: fully tax-free.
  • Payable on: Resignation, retirement, termination, death, disablement

FAQ

What if I leave at 4.5 years?

No gratuity. The 5-year rule is strict (one judgement held 4 years 240 days = eligible, but rare).

Is gratuity part of CTC?

Yes, but it doesn't reach your bank monthly. Treat it as deferred payout.

Tax on amount over ₹20 lakh?

Taxable at your slab rate in the year of receipt.
