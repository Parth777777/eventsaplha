Why CTC ≠ take-home

  • Employer PF (12% of basic): goes to your EPF account, NOT in your bank
  • Gratuity (~4.81% of basic): employer sets aside, payable on resignation after 5+ years
  • Employee PF (12% of basic): deducted from your salary
  • Professional tax: ~₹200/month in most states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, WB, Gujarat)
  • Income tax (TDS): based on annual taxable income + chosen regime

FAQ

Is gratuity part of in-hand?

No. Gratuity is paid only on resignation after 5+ years (or at retirement). Most companies show it in CTC but it never lands in your bank monthly.

Why is employee PF deducted if it's "my money"?

It is your money — saved for retirement. It earns 8.25% tax-free. Treat it as forced savings, not a tax.

Old or new regime — which to pick at offer?

New regime if your tax-saving investments are < ₹4 lakh. Old if more. Switching is free each year for salaried.
