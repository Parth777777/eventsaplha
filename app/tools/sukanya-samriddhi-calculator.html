SSY rules quick reference

  • Eligibility: One account per daughter, max two daughters (third allowed only if 2nd is twins). Open before age 10.
  • Deposits: 15 years from account opening. Min ₹250/yr, max ₹1.5L/yr (counts toward 80C).
  • Maturity: When daughter turns 21, OR on her marriage after age 18.
  • Partial withdrawal: Up to 50% allowed after she turns 18, for education.
  • Tax: EEE — 80C on contribution, interest tax-free, maturity tax-free.

FAQ

SSY vs PPF vs equity MF for daughter's education?

SSY: highest tax-free rate (8.2%) but locked till 21. PPF: 7.1%, more flexible. Equity MF (SIP): higher expected return but volatile. Balanced: max SSY + small equity SIP.

What if I don't deposit one year?

Account becomes "discontinued" — pay ₹50 penalty + minimum ₹250 to revive.

Where to open SSY?

Any post office or designated bank (SBI, PNB, ICICI, HDFC, etc.). KYC + daughter's birth certificate.
