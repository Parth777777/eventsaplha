Compound interest formula

FV = P × (1 + r/k)k×n

Where P = principal, r = annual rate, k = compounding periods per year, n = years. Daily compounding (k=365) gives an effective annual rate slightly higher than the nominal — e.g. 8% nominal daily compounds to 8.33% effective.

Why compounding frequency matters

At 8% over 10 years on ₹1 lakh: yearly compounds to ₹2.16 L, quarterly to ₹2.21 L, monthly to ₹2.22 L, daily to ₹2.23 L. The gap looks small in 1 year but is meaningful over 20+ years, especially at higher rates.

FAQ

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is computed only on the principal; compound interest is computed on principal + accumulated interest. Over long periods compound interest absolutely dominates.

Which compounding frequency is best?

Higher is better for the investor. Indian FDs typically compound quarterly. Savings accounts compute daily but credit quarterly. NPS / PPF compound annually.

How is effective annual rate (EAR) calculated?

EAR = (1 + r/k)k − 1. For 12% compounded monthly, EAR = (1.01)^12 − 1 = 12.68%.
