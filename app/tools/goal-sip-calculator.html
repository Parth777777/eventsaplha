The reverse SIP formula

SIP = FV × (r/12) / [ ((1 + r/12)12n − 1) × (1 + r/12) ]

This is the SIP formula rearranged to solve for the monthly contribution. The more time you have, the smaller the monthly amount you need — that's compounding at work.

Goal planning examples (12% return assumption)

  • ₹50 lakh in 15 years — about ₹10,000/month
  • ₹1 crore in 20 years — about ₹10,100/month (yes, that's how powerful compounding is)
  • ₹2 crore in 25 years — about ₹10,600/month
  • ₹5 crore in 30 years — about ₹17,400/month

Start early — every 5 years of delay roughly doubles the required SIP.

FAQ

Should I adjust the goal for inflation?

Yes. A ₹40 lakh goal today is ₹80 lakh in 12 years at 6% inflation. Use our Inflation Calculator to compute the inflated target before plugging it in here.

Can I use a step-up SIP to reach the goal with a smaller starting amount?

Yes — see the Step-up SIP Calculator. A 10% annual increase often cuts the starting SIP by 30-40%.

What return rate is safe to assume?

For long horizons (15+ yrs) in equity mutual funds, 11-12% is widely used. For shorter horizons (3-7 yrs), 9-10% with hybrid funds. For < 3 yrs use debt at 6-7%.
