FAQ

How does the ₹1.25 L equity LTCG exemption work?

Per financial year, total equity LTCG up to ₹1.25 lakh is tax-free. Above that, 12.5% on the excess.

Is indexation still available?

For property and gold sold after Jul 23 2024 you have a choice: 12.5% without indexation OR 20% with indexation (older purchases). For debt MF post-Apr-2023, no indexation.

How are mutual fund SIPs taxed?

Each instalment is a separate buy. FIFO determines order. Use our MF Return Calculator for SIP-style tax estimate.
