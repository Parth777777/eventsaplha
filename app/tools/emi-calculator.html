EMI formula

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)n] / [(1+r)n − 1]

Where P = principal, r = monthly interest rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = number of months. The same formula works for any flat-EMI loan: personal, car, education, etc. Home loans use the same formula but with longer tenures — see the dedicated Home Loan Calculator.

FAQ

Does EMI include processing fees?

No. Lenders typically charge 0.5-2% as processing fee, deducted upfront. Add that to your effective cost.

What if interest rate changes mid-tenure (floating rate)?

Either the EMI stays and tenure changes, or tenure stays and EMI changes. Most retail loans default to extending tenure.

Is prepayment allowed without penalty?

RBI rules: floating-rate retail loans cannot levy prepayment penalty. Fixed-rate loans can have a 2-4% prepayment charge. Always check the sanction letter.
