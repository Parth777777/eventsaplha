FAQ

Old or new regime — when is each better?

If your total deductions (80C + HRA + home loan + NPS + 80D) exceed ~₹4 lakh, old usually wins. Below that, new regime almost always wins.

Can I switch between regimes every year?

Salaried: yes, switch every year. Business income earners can switch only once in a lifetime back to old.

Is HRA available in new regime?

No. HRA, LTA, home loan interest on self-occupied, 80C, 80D — all unavailable in new regime.
