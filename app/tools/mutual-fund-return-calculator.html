Mutual fund tax in India (FY 2025-26)

  • Equity-oriented MF: STCG (held < 12 months) taxed at 20%. LTCG over ₹1.25 lakh / year taxed at 12.5%.
  • Debt MF (post Apr 2023 purchase): all gains taxed at investor's slab rate (treated as STCG, no LTCG benefit).
  • Hybrid MF (≥ 65% equity): treated as equity for tax purposes.

This calculator estimates tax assuming the entire gain is realised at sale. For an accurate slab-based debt-fund tax, use the Capital Gains Tax Calculator.

FAQ

Why is my actual return different from this?

This is a buy-and-sell calculator. If you did SIPs across multiple dates, use XIRR (each AMC's statement shows XIRR).

Is the LTCG exemption per year or per fund?

Per financial year, total across all equity LTCG. Plan redemptions across years to use the ₹1.25 lakh exemption multiple times.

How are SIP investments taxed?

Each SIP instalment is treated as a separate buy. FIFO accounting determines which units are sold and how long each was held.
