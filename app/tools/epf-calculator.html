How EPF contributions work

  • Employee: 12% of basic + DA goes to EPF
  • Employer: 12% of basic + DA matched — but split: 8.33% to EPS (Employee Pension Scheme, capped at ₹15k basic) + 3.67% to EPF
  • Effective to EPF account: 12% (you) + 3.67% (employer) = ~15.67% of basic — this calculator uses that
  • Tax status (FY25-26): Interest tax-free up to ₹2.5 lakh / year contribution. Above that, interest on excess is taxable

FAQ

Can I withdraw EPF before retirement?

Partial withdrawal allowed for home purchase, medical, marriage, education. Full withdrawal on resignation after 2 months of unemployment (taxable if EPF < 5 yrs old).

Can I contribute more than 12%?

Yes — Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF) lets you contribute up to 100% of basic. Same EPF rate applies. Tax-free until contribution + employer match crosses ₹2.5L/yr.

What happens to EPF when I switch jobs?

Transfer the UAN to the new employer. Don't withdraw — keep compounding tax-free.
