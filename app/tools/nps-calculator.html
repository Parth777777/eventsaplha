NPS tax benefits

  • Section 80CCD(1): Employee contribution up to 10% of salary (within ₹1.5L 80C limit) — old regime
  • Section 80CCD(1B): Additional ₹50,000 over and above 80C — old regime only
  • Section 80CCD(2): Employer contribution up to 14% of basic — available in BOTH regimes
  • At maturity: 60% lumpsum is tax-free; annuity income taxed at slab

FAQ

NPS Tier I or Tier II?

Tier I: tax benefits + 60-year lock. Tier II: liquid, no tax benefit. Use Tier I for retirement, Tier II as a flexible parking.

Why is 40% annuity mandatory?

Regulatory rule to ensure NPS members have lifelong pension income post-retirement.

Asset allocation in NPS?

Auto choice (age-based glide path) or Active choice (you pick equity/corporate bonds/govt securities up to 75% equity before age 50).
